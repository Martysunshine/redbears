---
title: apksigner
platform: android
source: https://developer.android.com/tools/apksigner
---

apksigner, available in revision 24.0.3 and higher of the Android SDK Build Tools, is contained in the @MASTG-TOOL-0006 at `[SDK-Path]/build-tools/[version]/apksigner` and can be used to verify APK signatures, rotate keys and display additional information about the signing certificates and keys.
